Privacy Policy – my-cmp-youtube

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Who uses this tool

my-cmp-youtube is used only by the owner of a YouTube channel, on their own computer, for that channel only. No other users are accepted.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube), the tool reads the list of the owner's own videos and their settings (title, description, tags, privacy status, scheduled publish time and related flags), and updates those settings when the owner approves each change.

How the data is stored and shared

  • The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to third parties.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used on this site or by the tool.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account – Third-party apps & services.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
